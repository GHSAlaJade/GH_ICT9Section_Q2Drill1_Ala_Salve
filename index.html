What is Volleyball?

Volleyball is a cool team sport played with a net in the middle. Two teams of 6 players try to hit a ball over the net so it lands on the opponent's side! You only get 3 hits to return the ball over the net!

  • Played on a court measuring 18m x 9m
  • Net height is around 2.43m for men and 2.24m for women
  • Points are scored when the ball touches the court floor inside bounds!

Basic Moves Every Beginner Should Know

1. Serving

This is how you start the rally! You can do an underhand serve (easy for beginners) or an overhand serve (much harder!).

Tip: Always keep your eyes on the ball before hitting it!

2. Passing (Bumping)

You use your forearms together to control the ball when the other team serves or spikes it to your side.

Tip: Don't swing your arms too high or the ball goes backwards!

3. Setting & Spiking

Setting uses your fingertips overhead to push the ball up high. Spiking is jumping and smashing it down hard!

Tip: Practice your jump timing to hit at the highest point!

Quick Rules Checklist 📋

  1. No hitting the ball twice in a row by the same person!
  2. Maximum of 3 hits per side before going over the net.
  3. Don't touch the net with your body while playing.
  4. Out of bounds is when the ball lands completely outside the lines.
  5. First team to reach 25 points (and win by 2) wins the set!
